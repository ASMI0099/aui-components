Copy buttons

copy-text copies the text with a click and shows a check and "Copied" for 2 seconds (a cross and "Copy failed" when the browser refuses); a text button keeps its width. copy-feedback="none" copies and keeps its look: here the page confirms with a notice. done-text shows the check after on-click's promise.

Loading, waiting, disabled

Loading keeps the width and the focus (Tab to a button, press Enter) and takes no click. on-click returning a promise waits by itself: no second click, no double submit. loading-text shows a text of its own meanwhile. Disabled keeps its tooltip.

Opening a row's variants (expanded)

expanded="row.open": the chevron points right while closed and turns down when open; the button flips the value itself. The variants come from the server on the first opening: on-click returns the request, the spinner shows in the chevron's place, and the row opens once they arrive ("Desk lamp" fails once: it stays closed).
