The whole tree by load-options, in one response (no paging)

As a form whose categories come all at once: load-options answers every node in one response, as a flat list (/categories/flat: part of the catalog, or a larger generated tree; each node has its parentId, parent-field builds the tree), on the first open, then reused (cache-ttl). The panel opens at once with its loading look; the rows follow the answer.
