show-count

show-count (off unless set) shows the length of the text at the end of the box, out of value-max-length when that is set: 3/7. It counts what the limit counts (the text without the spaces around it); longer than the limit, the count and the field turn red at once. The clear button stands before it. With enforce-limits (off unless set) the box stops at the limit instead (a longer paste is cut).

Numbers

input-type="number" with value-max-length: digits only, no more than the limit (typing over a selection replaces it); keys that type nothing (arrows, Home/End, F5, Esc) work. Emptied or cleared, the field is just empty (no error). A numeric text from the page ("12") shows as its number. A typed number field: no browser spin buttons, and the mouse wheel over a focused field leaves it and scrolls the page (it never steps the value); ArrowUp / ArrowDown step a field without a digit limit. With enforce-limits, a key or paste that would take the number above max (or below a min under zero) is refused; without it, the field shows the max error.

Message line, size, label on top (aui-input, aui-select, aui-tree-select)

hint-text puts a hint on a line under the field; once the field shows invalid, the line says why: error-messages (a text per check, {length}, {min}, {max} filled in), error-text (one text), or with show-errors the shared texts, which a page sets once (auiFieldMessagesProvider.set). The line keeps its room, so nothing moves. size="small" / "large": 28 / 40px. label-position="top": the label above the box. Leave a field to see its message.

aui-textarea

The options, as the selects read them

A bare attribute is on (show-label, readonly, clearable, disabled, show-count), as on aui-select, and so is one written the HTML way (disabled="disabled"); an expression or "true" / "false" counts as given and follows its changes.
